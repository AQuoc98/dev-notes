## Readiness gates

The candidate is ready for active senior-level interview loops when all are true:

- [ ] Two timed practical coding problems are completed in 45 minutes with
      clarification, narration, tests, and complexity discussion.
- [ ] Two different frontend systems can be designed in 45 minutes with clear
      assumptions, trade-offs, failure modes, security, and observability.
- [ ] React rendering and Next.js server/client, rendering, and caching choices
      can be explained with concrete consequences.
- [ ] A real performance issue can be diagnosed from measurement rather than
      intuition.
- [ ] The capstone has a stable demo and the evidence package is complete.
- [ ] Eight behavioral stories are concise, specific, and include reflection.
- [ ] No critical mistake repeats in the two most recent mock loops.
- [ ] The candidate has questions for evaluating engineering culture, role
      scope, delivery practices, and expectations of senior engineers.

Review conversion every two weeks:

- Few recruiter screens: revise targeting, resume evidence, and referrals.
- Screens but no technical rounds: improve role fit and project communication.
- Technical rounds but no finals: classify gaps by coding, depth, design, or
  communication and schedule corrective mocks.
- Finals but no offers: examine senior-level scope, behavioral evidence,
  trade-off quality, and company-specific expectations.
