# Senior Frontend Interview Preparation Path

A 16-week plan for moving from a mid-level React engineer toward a Senior
Frontend Developer role, with React and Next.js as the primary stack.

## Working assumptions

Until the candidate profile is refined, this plan assumes:

- product companies or remote/multinational teams;
- interviews may be conducted in English;
- Next.js App Router knowledge needs production-level reinforcement;
- frontend system design and live explanation are the highest-value gaps;
- 40% of the time is spent learning and reviewing, and 60% producing,
  implementing, explaining, or interviewing;
- a sustainable baseline of 14 hours per week (224 hours total).

Use [00-profile-and-baseline.md](./00-profile-and-baseline.md) to replace these
assumptions after the first diagnostic.

6. [Progress tracker](./05-progress-tracker.md) — weekly scorecard and readiness

## How to use the plan

1. Complete the baseline in Week 0 or the first two days of Week 1.
2. Put the recurring study blocks on the calendar before starting.
3. Follow the weekly roadmap, but protect the deliverable and exit criterion
   when time is limited. Reading is the first thing to reduce.
4. Update the tracker every Sunday. Carry at most one unfinished item into the
   next week.
5. Start applications in Week 11; do not wait until the entire plan is complete.

The target is not exhaustive knowledge. It is repeatable evidence that the
candidate can make sound trade-offs, deliver reliable frontend systems, and
communicate decisions at senior level.
