# Subtask 12: Create a GA4 Operations and Scenario Runbook

## Objective

Document how to operate the GA4 property after collection is implemented and how to configure, test, and monitor common measurement scenarios.

## Scope — Included Items

- Property, stream, access, enhanced-measurement, data-quality, privacy, and retention decisions.
- SPA, cross-domain, unwanted referrals, internal/developer traffic, campaigns, authenticated users, ecommerce, and offline/server events.
- Reporting/export readiness and operational change control.

## Deliverables / Outputs

- One GA4 property operations runbook containing the account/property/stream map, ownership/access matrix, timezone, currency, collection settings, data filters, key events, custom definitions, privacy/retention/deletion decisions, product links, reporting context, exports, and change/incident log.
- One common-scenarios document containing decision records, configuration location, owner, risks, and test evidence for each applicable SPA, enhanced-measurement, cross-domain, unwanted-referral, internal/developer-traffic, campaign, User-ID, ecommerce, and Measurement Protocol scenario.
- One operational handover checklist covering monitoring, periodic access/configuration review, irreversible-change approval, incident response, and sanitized evidence storage.

## Dependencies

- GA4 Editor/Administrator support.
- Approved measurement plan, privacy requirements, and GTM implementation.
