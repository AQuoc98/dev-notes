# Subtask 12: Create a GA4 Operations and Scenario Runbook

## Objective

Document how to operate the GA4 property after collection is implemented and how to configure, test, and monitor common measurement scenarios.

## Scope

- Property, stream, access, enhanced-measurement, data-quality, privacy, and retention decisions.
- SPA, cross-domain, unwanted referrals, internal/developer traffic, campaigns, authenticated users, ecommerce, and offline/server events.
- Reporting/export readiness and operational change control.

## Work Items

1. Record account/property/stream structure, owners, timezone, currency, access, and links.
2. Inventory property and stream settings that affect collection and reporting.
3. Complete applicable scenario decision records.
4. Define testing, monitoring, mitigation, and periodic review.
5. Create a sanitized operational handover checklist.

## Deliverables / Outputs

- GA4 property operations checklist.
- Scenario decision records and evidence.
- Ownership/access matrix and change log.
- Data-quality, privacy, retention, and export decisions.

## Expected Result

The team can explain where GA4 behavior is configured, choose the correct pattern for common scenarios, and operate changes without silently corrupting future data.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Property/stream settings, owners, roles, timezone, currency, and destinations are documented.
- [ ] Enhanced measurement and duplicate-risk decisions are recorded.
- [ ] Applicable attribution, traffic, identity, ecommerce, and server-event cases are addressed.
- [ ] Irreversible or prospective-only settings are tested and approved before activation.
- [ ] Privacy, retention, deletion, reporting, and export implications are documented.

## Dependencies

- GA4 Editor/Administrator support.
- Approved measurement plan, privacy requirements, and GTM implementation.

## Estimated Effort

**10 hours** — inventory 2h, scenario decisions 4h, testing 2h, runbook/review 2h.
