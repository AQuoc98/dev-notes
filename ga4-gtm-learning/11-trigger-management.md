# Subtask 11: Standardize GTM Trigger Management

## Objective

Define how GTM triggers are selected, scoped, named, tested, reused, and retired so tags fire at the intended business moment exactly as designed.

## Scope — Included Items

- Page View, Initialization, Consent Initialization, Custom Event, click, form, history change, timer, and trigger group usage where applicable.
- Firing filters, exceptions, event timing, shared triggers, and dependencies.
- The selected flow/POC rather than a full-container rewrite.

## Deliverables / Outputs

- One GTM trigger management guideline containing trigger-type selection, naming, event/filter design, timing, consent/initialization rules, exceptions, trigger groups, reuse, testing, change control, and retirement rules.
- One scoped trigger inventory and consumer map containing type/event, conditions, consuming tags, exceptions/group membership, timing risk, owner, and status.
- One trigger decision matrix and completed test record covering positive, negative, duplicate, SPA/navigation, consent, exception, group-order, and downstream tag behavior.

## Dependencies

- Approved Data Layer contract and event naming standard.
- Scoped tag inventory and GTM Preview access.
