# Subtask 09: Design, Build, and Interpret GA4 Reports and Charts

## Objective

Learn how to turn validated GA4 data into reusable Reports and analysis-oriented Explorations that answer explicit business questions and support documented decisions.

## Scope — Included Items

- Dimensions, metrics, scope, date ranges, filters, comparisons, segments, key events, and attribution context.
- Report readiness: standard fields, custom dimensions/metrics, registration timing, cardinality, and privacy risk.
- GA4 Reports workspace: overview/detail reports, charts, summary cards, collections, customization, and publishing.
- Explorations: free-form, funnel, path, cohort, and other relevant techniques.
- Chart selection, interpretation, QA, sharing, documentation, and maintenance ownership.
- Limitations including processing delay, thresholding, sampling, incompatible fields, and `(other)`/cardinality effects.

## Scope — Excluded Items

- A complete reporting suite for every stakeholder.
- External BI dashboards such as Looker Studio or Power BI.
- Business conclusions unsupported by validated tracking data or an agreed analytical question.

## Deliverables / Outputs

- One report-requirements matrix containing audience, question, decision, dimensions, metrics, scope, filters, surface, owner, and cadence.
- One field-readiness inventory for standard and custom dimensions/metrics.
- One configured reusable GA4 detail report and one configured free-form Exploration based on validated data.
- One chart-selection and interpretation guide.
- One report QA and interpretation document recording freshness, identity, attribution, thresholds, sampling, cardinality, discrepancies, and limitations.

## Dependencies

- Completed measurement plan and validated event flow.
- Sufficient data in the correct GA4 property and web stream.
- GA4 Editor or Administrator access for report customization, or an approved operator with that access.
- Stakeholder agreement on the questions, decisions, audience, and reporting cadence.
